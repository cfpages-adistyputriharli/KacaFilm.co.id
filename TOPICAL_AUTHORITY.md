# Topical Authority — kacafilm.co.id

## Role and boundary

KacaFilm.co.id should become an Indonesian reference for applied window films on vehicles and buildings: product construction, optical/solar performance, UV/fading, privacy, safety/security, glass compatibility, legal and operational constraints, survey/specification, estimating, installation, defect diagnosis, care, removal, procurement, and contractor evidence.

The site may cover automotive and architectural film independently because readers, regulations, glass systems, installation conditions, and commercial routes differ. It does not own general glass engineering, façade design, automotive glazing replacement, alarm/security systems, or HVAC design. Kaca.co.id and other owned domains may independently cover the same subject; cross-domain overlap is allowed. Cannibalization applies only within KacaFilm.co.id.

## Existing coverage and risks

- Half the sitemap is local templates; city pages should not survive merely through generic service-area text.
- Legacy articles frequently compete for “choose film,” “best brand,” “types,” “benefits,” and installation intent.
- Marketing metrics such as IR rejection, UV rejection, total solar performance, darkness, privacy, security, “anti panas,” and warranty can be misinterpreted. Publications require test-method context and product-specific documentation.
- Automotive visibility/legal requirements and glass thermal-stress compatibility are high-stakes and must use current primary sources and qualified review.
- Brand comparison pages risk stale specifications, unfair unsupported claims, and trademark confusion.

- Every child links to its hub; no location page enters generic related blocks.

## Evidence and editorial standards

Use product-specific manufacturer reports and current Indonesian primary legal sources. Explain test method, glazing, wavelength/range, specimen, and limitations before repeating performance numbers. Never invent VLT, TSER, IR/UV rejection, darkness legality, energy savings, security, breakage, warranty, price, or life claims. Thermal stress, safety/security, façade, vehicle visibility/electronics, and removal around defoggers require qualified review.
